MARKETING COURSE PROJECT IN PROGRESS

Unlimited Flight Pass for HK Express

We proposed a new unlimited flight pass for HK Express targeting Hong Kong and Asia travellers, including Gen Z and millennial leisure travellers. The idea breaks away from traditional travel restrictions that require months of advance planning: travellers can book tickets and depart immediately when the weather is nice on a weekend or when a sudden two-day holiday comes up.

My role: Target market research.
